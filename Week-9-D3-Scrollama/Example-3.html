Massachusetts, county by county

A Scrollama + D3 example: the county shapes on a map turn into the bars of a bar chart as you scroll.

Loading the map...

Massachusetts has 14 counties. Their sizes on the map tell us nothing about how many people live in each.

Color each county by its population in the 2020 Census. Darker means more people. Middlesex stands out.

Now lift every county off the map and line them up from most to least populous. Each shape is resized to fit the same slot.

Each shape morphs into a bar. The height of the bar is the county's population.

The four largest counties hold most of the state's 7 million people.

Back on the map: Middlesex, Essex and Suffolk sit around Boston in the east, and Worcester fills the center. The western counties are large in area but small in population.

Data: U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Census county population counts. County shapes: us-atlas (U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundaries).
